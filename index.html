About Justice Desk

Four Decades of Defining Counsel

Founded in a single Manhattan office by Eleanor Sterling and Henry Vale in 1984, the firm began with a conviction: that the law, practised brilliantly, is the most powerful instrument of human progress.

Today, Justice Desk is a focused legal services practice with 8 senior attorneys based at our New York headquarters. Yet our founding ethos remains untouched — every client receives partner-level attention, every matter receives uncompromising preparation, and every outcome is pursued with surgical precision.

We measure success in the futures we secure — the companies built, the liberties defended, the legacies protected. Every client receives partner-level attention and a clear plan, from your first call to your final relief.
