FAQ

Family Law, Answered

An uncontested divorce can be finalised in roughly 4 to 6 months. Contested matters — custody, support, property — typically run longer. At your consultation we'll give you an honest timeline based on your specific circumstances.
Many family matters resolve through negotiation or mediation, keeping you out of the courtroom entirely. If an agreement isn't possible, we will prepare you completely and advocate for you before a judge — but court is never our first choice.
Courts focus on the child's best interests — stability, parental involvement and existing routines. We help you present a strong, evidence-based parenting plan, and negotiate arrangements that work for your family rather than against it.
Most matters begin with a free consultation. Uncontested divorces are billed as a flat fee agreed in writing up front. Contested matters carry a transparent, capped fee structure — so you always know where you stand.
