The Firm

Forty Years of
Defining Counsel

Founded in a single Manhattan office by Eleanor Sterling and Henry Vale, the firm began with a conviction: that the law, practised brilliantly, is the most powerful instrument of human progress.

Today, Sterling & Vale is an international powerhouse with 45 senior attorneys across 6 global offices. Yet our founding ethos remains untouched — every client receives partner-level attention, every matter receives uncompromising preparation, and every outcome is pursued with surgical precision.

We measure success not in cases won, but in futures secured — the companies built, the liberties defended, and the legacies protected.

Milestones

The Journey
So Far

1984

The Founding

Eleanor Sterling and Henry Vale open a two-desks office on Madison Avenue with a single case — and a refusal to lose it.

1992

First Landmark Victory

Our firm secures a precedent-setting corporate ruling that reshapes merger law in New York State.

2001

International Expansion

Offices open in London and Hong Kong, establishing our global dispute resolution practice.

2013

Chambers Band 1 Recognition

First of our decade-long unbroken run as a Band 1 firm for Corporate Law.

2021

Digital Justice Initiative

We pioneer a secure client case portal and AI-assisted research unit — a first for a firm of our scale.

2026

A Legacy Continued

Now 45 senior attorneys across 6 offices, still bound by the founding promise: never lose a case that deserves to be won.
